Question 2: what the wrong answers were

“Given y = 3/x, is y directly proportional to x?” — How the 23 students answered:

No, because y falls as x rises (correct)15
Yes, because y is always worked out from x6
Yes, because 3 is the constant2
All eight who got it wrong chose an option starting with “Yes”. They are reading any formula that links two quantities as direct proportion. When going over it, have them build a table with x = 1, 2, 3 and fill in y themselves. Once they see y coming down, they spot the mistake without the definition being restated.
